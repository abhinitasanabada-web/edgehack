# Startup overload

## Symptoms
The endpoint is slow immediately after sign-in.

## Evidence to collect
Startup processes, memory use, disk activity, and recent updates.

## Likely causes
Too many startup programs or a pending update.

## Approved fixes
No automatic startup configuration changes in this prototype.

## Verification steps
Compare startup telemetry after an administrator-approved change.

## When to escalate
Escalate when a protected service or update is implicated.
