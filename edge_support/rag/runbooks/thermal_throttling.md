# Thermal throttling

## Symptoms
Performance drops while temperature or fan activity is high.

## Evidence to collect
Temperature if available, CPU load, power state, and process list.

## Likely causes
Blocked airflow, sustained workload, ambient heat, or cooling failure.

## Approved fixes
Collect more telemetry; do not alter firmware or power settings automatically.

## Verification steps
Measure temperature and performance after reducing workload.

## When to escalate
Escalate for critical temperature or hardware symptoms.
