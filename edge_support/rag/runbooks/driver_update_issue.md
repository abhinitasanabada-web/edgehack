# Driver or update issue

## Symptoms
Hardware behavior changed after an update or device errors appear.

## Evidence to collect
Device name, update timing, event summary, and rollback availability.

## Likely causes
Incompatible driver, incomplete update, or hardware fault.

## Approved fixes
No automatic rollback or driver installation.

## Verification steps
Use vendor-approved diagnostics after human review.

## When to escalate
Escalate all driver changes and suspected hardware failures.
