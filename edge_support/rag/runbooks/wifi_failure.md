# Wi-Fi failure

## Symptoms
The endpoint is disconnected or has unstable wireless connectivity.

## Evidence to collect
Adapter status, signal quality, gateway reachability, and recent network errors.

## Likely causes
Weak signal, disabled adapter, access-point outage, or authentication failure.

## Approved fixes
Collect more telemetry; do not change wireless profiles automatically.

## Verification steps
Confirm adapter and gateway state after any approved action.

## When to escalate
Escalate for credentials, enterprise policy, hardware, or repeated disconnects.
