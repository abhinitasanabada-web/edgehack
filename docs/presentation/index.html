Who it is for

A Tier-1 help-desk technician at a site full of employee laptops

The job

Most tickets are the same dozen problems: CPU or memory pressure, a full disk, heat, battery wear, Wi-Fi, DNS, a crashing app, slow boots. Each needs someone to read device telemetry and match it to a runbook.

The catch

That telemetry and those logs carry e-mails, user paths, internal IPs and sometimes secrets. And the most common ticket, "websites won't load", is exactly when a cloud assistant can't be reached.
