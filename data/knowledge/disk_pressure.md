# DEMO / SAMPLE: Low disk space and disk pressure

Category: disk_pressure

A nearly full disk can prevent updates and paging. Review storage usage. Remove only files you own and know are disposable, after backup verification. Use the operating system storage cleanup interface. Never remove system folders or unknown enterprise data. Escalate storage errors or suspected data loss.

Source: synthetic hackathon knowledge, not official HP guidance. Thresholds are illustrative and require fleet-specific validation.
