# DEMO / SAMPLE: DNS and network troubleshooting

Category: dns_network

Distinguish Wi-Fi connectivity from DNS name resolution. Check if other approved services work and compare a hostname with approved diagnostics. Verify VPN status and capture the exact name-resolution error. Do not switch corporate DNS or disable firewall or VPN policy. Escalate persistent DNS or enterprise routing failures.
