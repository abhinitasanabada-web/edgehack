# DEMO / SAMPLE: Battery degradation and short runtime

Category: battery_degradation

Low reported battery health may explain short runtime but depends on vendor measurement. Check the manufacturer battery report and compare runtime under similar workload. Use approved power accessories. Swelling, unusual heat or physical damage requires stopping use and contacting support. Battery replacement should follow authorized service procedures.

Source: synthetic hackathon knowledge, not official HP guidance. Thresholds are illustrative and require fleet-specific validation.
