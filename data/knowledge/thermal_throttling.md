# DEMO / SAMPLE: Overheating and thermal throttling

Category: thermal_throttling

High temperature with poor performance may indicate thermal throttling, but device-specific limits vary. Place the laptop on a hard ventilated surface, check vents without opening the device, and reduce workload. Burning smell, smoke or swelling requires stopping use and contacting support immediately. Do not open the battery enclosure.

Source: synthetic hackathon knowledge, not official HP guidance. Thresholds are illustrative and require fleet-specific validation.
